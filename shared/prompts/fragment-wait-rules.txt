WAITING: never compose a poll of your own — no `sleep` loops, no `pgrep -f` (a pattern in your own command line matches the waiting shell itself and never goes false; discussion #527). To wait for the checks on your pull request run `{{BIN}}/wait-for.sh checks {{REPO}} <pr-number>` (returns when they settle, or after its budget); to wait for a process you started run `{{BIN}}/wait-for.sh pid <pid>`. For a wait longer than this session should hold open — a check run that takes longer than your remaining budget — run `{{BIN}}/park-until.sh checks {{REPO}} <pr-number>` and END your session: the engine owns the wait from there and dispatches you again, resumed, the moment the checks conclude, with `why=wait#{{REPO}}#<pr-number>` on the record.
