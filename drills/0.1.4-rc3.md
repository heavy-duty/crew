  candidate.

  the creds-free rows have read them logged out. With a `tg:` entry, the
  notify leg reads a real channel for the first time.

  must end with that box disarmed and its clock back on the host's; the leg
  fails if not.

  staying up (the round caps its fleet at its roster size, #784) and the
  restored boot's own gate passing (#785).
